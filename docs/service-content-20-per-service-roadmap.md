# 20-page-per-service tracker

Target: about 20 distinct, useful landing pages in each service cluster, counting the existing service hub as one. The supporting pages are informational landing pages under `blog/` (and their English equivalents under `en/blog/`), each answering a different reader task and linking to its relevant service hub. Supporting pages count only when their intent is distinct and relevant to that hub. A genuinely shared guide remains one canonical URL and may be assigned to more than one relevant cluster.

This is a staged publishing target, not permission to publish thin pages to satisfy a number. Each page needs a distinct reader task, evidence, and professional review for practice-specific claims.

That is 341 of 400 target cluster-page assignments, leaving 59 assignments. The table below counts article routes currently linked from each hub; a shared route counts once in every relevant cluster.
