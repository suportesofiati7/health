## Workflow for future pages

1. Record the precise public-facing claim and the page where it will appear.
2. Identify evidence type, population, device/protocol, outcomes and limitations; prefer guidelines and systematic reviews, then relevant primary studies and manufacturer manuals for device specifications.
3. Separate general scientific information from a claim about Franciele’s actual equipment or practice.
4. Ask the professional to verify every practice-specific protocol, availability, scope and instruction.
5. Keep a dated source note with author/title, publication, direct URL, access date and evidence caveat.
6. Remove or qualify claims that are unsupported, outside verified scope, or too device-specific to generalize.
