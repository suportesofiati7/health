# Londrina growth plan

## Positioning

Franciele Sofiati is a consultation-led aesthetics practice in Londrina. The
site should be the clearest local source for realistic preparation, treatment
selection, recovery and expectations—not a catalogue of promises.

## First 90 days

1. Verify the exact address, category, phone, appointment URL and current
   review link in Google Business Profile. Request a review after a completed
   experience, never in exchange for a benefit.
2. Publish one Portuguese article each week. Each article answers one patient
   question and links to one service page and the consultation page.
3. Publish one short clinical video or carousel per article on Instagram,
   then link it back to the full site page.
4. Review Search Console weekly: queries with impressions but low click-through
   deserve stronger titles/descriptions; queries ranking on page two deserve a
   clearer page section or supporting article.

## Local reach without misleading city pages

Keep the physical location and the structured-data address in Londrina. Use
regional content only where it is useful, such as travel-to-consultation
guidance for Cambé, Ibiporã, Rolândia, Arapongas and Apucarana. Do not create
pages claiming offices or local teams in cities where Franciele does not
actually practise.

## Reputation

Quote reviews exactly, identify them as Google reviews, and send readers to
the source. Do not copy Google rating totals into schema. Use review themes
(clarity, careful explanation, personalised care, sensitivity to comfort) as
topics for service-page FAQs and educational posts, never as guarantees.
