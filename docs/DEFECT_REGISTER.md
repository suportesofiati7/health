## Severity guide

- **Critical:** data loss, unauthorized clinical disclosure, or a production outage with no safe workaround.
- **High:** important user workflow/security/build failure. Tooling-only dependency advisories are labeled separately from production runtime risk.
- **Medium:** user-facing navigation, content quality, delivery uncertainty or incomplete verification with a safe workaround.
- **Low:** maintainability or lower-impact behavior without evidence of production failure.
