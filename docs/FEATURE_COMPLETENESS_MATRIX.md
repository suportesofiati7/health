## Explicitly restricted or intentionally absent operations

- Finalized clinical notes and procedures are immutable to preserve clinical history. Drafts are editable. The new procedure workflow resumes plan-generated drafts and finalizes them with inventory usage in the same database transaction. A formal amendment workflow for correcting a mistaken finalized procedure remains a product/compliance decision.
- Posted financial and inventory records are corrected with refunds, reversals or adjustment entries, not destructive edits/deletes.
- Patient permanent deletion is a restricted RPC that removes dependent data and private files only under its authorization and safety rules. It is not equivalent to archive and is not available to every role.
- Consent decisions, audit history, communication history, and database migrations are retained/versioned by design.
- Treatment-plan-item editing/deletion, complete product/lot CRUD, and an expanded recoverable archive are not established requirements in the current UI. Their dependency and retention rules need review before destructive controls are added.
