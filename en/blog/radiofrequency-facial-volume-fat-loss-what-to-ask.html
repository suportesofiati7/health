Can radiofrequency change facial volume? What to ask about the device

“Radiofrequency” describes a family of technologies, not one device or delivery method. An FDA safety communication reports serious complications with certain uses of RF microneedling; it does not establish that every modality has the same risk.

First, confirm which type of radiofrequency is proposed

Radiofrequency can be delivered through different technologies and applicators. Microneedling devices pass electrodes through the skin; other systems deliver energy at the surface or through different routes. A brand name, MHz frequency or the word “radiofrequency” alone does not identify the model, authorized indication or treatment depth.

The local service page does not identify the device or applicator. This guide therefore does not assume that the clinic offers RF microneedling or intends to reduce facial fat.

What the FDA communication says about fat loss

In October 2025, the US FDA said it had received reports of burns, scarring, fat loss, disfigurement and nerve damage after certain uses of RF microneedling devices in dermatologic or aesthetic procedures. The FDA describes energy delivered through small needles and says its evaluation is ongoing.

Postmarket reports are a safety signal, not a measure of how often a problem occurs or proof that a device caused every reported event. The communication concerns RF microneedling in the United States; it should not automatically be applied to surface radiofrequency, every brand or Brazilian regulation. A 2026 review of complications also describes heterogeneous studies and evidence based largely on case series and retrospective studies.

Practical questions before deciding

  • What is the exact manufacturer and model of the device and applicator?
  • Is the proposed modality microneedling, noninvasive or something else? What intended use appears in the manufacturer’s instructions?
  • Which tissue or goal is being treated, and is there an intention to affect adipose tissue?
  • How do anatomy, facial volume, previous procedures and the planned area change the individual assessment?
  • What risks, alternatives and post-session signs require guidance or follow-up?

Ask for the modality and plan to be explained clearly, without relying only on before-and-after photos or a brand name. The professional should confirm the specific device’s Brazilian registration and instructions, the proposed use and professional scope. This page does not assess individual suitability.

Sources and limits

  • FDA (Oct. 15, 2025): potential risks with certain uses of RF microneedling.
  • 2026 review: complications of RF microneedling and limits in the available evidence.

The FDA notice does not identify the local device, quantify risk for the Londrina service or constitute Brazilian regulation. The model, applicator, indication and local instructions remain for the professional to confirm.
