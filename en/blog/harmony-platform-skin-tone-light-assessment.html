Harmony platform and skin tone: what needs to be identified?

The word “light” does not identify a modality, and studies for one indication do not answer questions about every other use.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Identify what is actually proposed

Platform, modality and target are different details

A platform may contain different energy sources or handpieces. Before interpreting a claim, ask whether the proposal uses a laser, intense pulsed light (IPL) or another modality, which handpiece will be used, and what target is being considered. The platform’s commercial name alone does not answer these questions.

02 · Skin tone belongs in assessment

Evidence must match the modality and indication

The AAD cautions that people with darker skin may be more prone to burns and dark marks after some laser treatments. A randomized trial of IPL for hair removal also found darker pigmentation and increasing fluence influenced adverse effects in that study. This does not predict an individual’s risk or establish safety or results for a Harmony device, a different handpiece, pigment or vascular target.

03 · Do not transfer settings between studies

Study context limits the conclusion

The studied modality, device, purpose, area, skin characteristics and protocol need to be comparable before applying a finding. Do not choose settings yourself or assume all light technologies share indications. Recent sun exposure, tanning, medicines and a history of pigment changes are useful details to disclose.

04 · Questions before deciding

Ask for an explanation that matches your proposal

  • What model, modality and handpiece are proposed?
  • What is the target, and what evidence applies to this specific indication?
  • How do skin tone, recent tanning and a history of dark marks affect assessment?
  • What limitations or alternatives should be considered?
  • Which written instructions and contact route apply?

Sources and limits

What these sources can help explain

The AAD guidance discusses lasers generally; the cited trial studied IPL hair removal, not the platform or indication proposed by this clinic. Local model, handpiece, purpose and regulatory status require practitioner confirmation before service-specific claims.
