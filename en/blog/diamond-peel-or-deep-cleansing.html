Diamond peel or deep cleansing: what does each service do?

Diamond microdermabrasion and deep cleansing can serve different goals: surface exfoliation and comedone extraction are not the same step. Learn what to ask before choosing.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Two services, different primary actions

Surface exfoliation is not comedone extraction

The local deep-cleansing page describes cleansing, skin preparation and careful comedone extraction when indicated. The diamond-peel page describes microdermabrasion using a diamond-tipped handpiece and suction to temporarily reduce superficial cells and improve texture. Their primary goals are therefore different, even though both may be discussed as facial-care services.

02 · What microdermabrasion does

The handpiece works at the skin surface

The American Academy of Dermatology describes microdermabrasion as a procedure that gently removes the outermost layer of skin. Diamond microdermabrasion uses an abrasive tip; the local service page also describes suction with the tip. This is not the same action as selecting and removing a comedone. Intensity and details depend on the device and protocol actually used.

03 · When the question is about blackheads

Selective extraction has its own criteria

The AAD explains that acne extraction uses sterilized instruments to remove blackheads and whiteheads in selected situations. This does not mean every visible lesion is a comedone, every cleansing session should include extraction, or that extraction treats the underlying cause of acne. If the main concern is persistent acne, inflammation, or a lesion whose nature is unclear, discuss appropriate assessment. Cosmetic cleansing does not replace acne diagnosis or treatment.

04 · Questions for a clearer choice

Confirm which step matches your concern

  • Is my main goal comedone extraction, surface exfoliation or another concern?
  • What does this specific service include: cleansing, emollience, extraction, an abrasive tip or suction?
  • If more than one step is planned, why, and how will my skin be assessed?
  • What is outside the service’s purpose, such as treating persistent acne or structural scars?
  • Which aspects of my current skin or history could change the plan?

The listed services have different descriptions and do not say that both techniques are combined in every visit. Confirm the details during assessment. Read about diamond peel and deep cleansing. For acne questions, also read why professional cleansing does not replace acne treatment.
