Botulinum toxin: natural results begin with anatomy and conversation

Natural results are not a single technique or a visual promise. A careful decision begins with how each person moves, what they hope to soften, and what they want to preserve in their expression.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Assess movement, not only lines

Assess movement, not only lines

Lines at rest and lines in motion do not tell exactly the same story. An assessment considers anatomy, asymmetries, goals, and the limits of a non-surgical approach.

02 · A temporary effect still deserves a careful decision

A temporary effect still deserves a careful decision

The duration of effect, whether a follow-up might be considered, discomfort, risks, and what to do if a question arises should be clear before treatment. A responsible result is not measured by a photograph alone.

03 · Choosing less can be valid

Choosing less can be valid

A plan may be gradual, may recommend waiting, or may conclude that this is not the best approach for the goal being discussed. Clarity before treatment helps protect trust afterwards.
