Scalp mesotherapy: questions about the product before an application

Learn what to ask about an injectable substance, its Brazilian regulatory status and the care explained before a procedure, without assuming which product a clinic uses.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Find out what is being proposed

A product name does not explain everything

“Mesotherapy” does not identify the product being applied. Before an application, it is reasonable to ask for its name, active ingredients, stated purpose, route of use and who will provide instructions. The proposal should be explained in clear language, including what is not yet known about benefit and risk for that indication.

02 · Regulatory status depends on intended use and route

Injectable products and cosmetics are not interchangeable categories

Anvisa states that products intended for injectable aesthetic procedures cannot be regularized as cosmetics; they must have a compatible status as medicines or medical devices. Packaging that appears sterile or can accept a needle does not, by itself, prove that the product is regularized for that purpose and route. Status needs to be checked for the exact product; the word “mesotherapy” does not reveal a clinic’s practice.

03 · Ask about risks and aftercare

Clear information belongs in the conversation

Anvisa guidance for aesthetic procedures recommends clarifying the product used, its ingredients, expiration date, aftercare and possible reactions or risks. The agency also distinguishes sanitary regulation of products from professional practice rules, which belong to the relevant professional councils. A general article therefore cannot determine qualifications, authorization or a local protocol; those details need confirmation from the responsible practitioner and applicable official sources.

04 · Confirm details with the responsible team

Practical questions before deciding

You can ask for the full product name and registration or regulatory status; its authorized purpose and route; the lot and expiration date; possible reactions; written aftercare instructions; and whom to contact if a problem occurs. If the answers are unclear, postpone the decision until you understand the proposal. Also read about scalp mesotherapy and evidence and differences between ingredients.
