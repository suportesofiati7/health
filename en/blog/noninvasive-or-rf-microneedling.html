Conventional or RF microneedling: what changes

Radiofrequency delivered through a surface electrode and RF microneedling are different procedures. Learn the distinction and questions about device, purpose and risk.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · RF is not one single technique

The energy pathway distinguishes the techniques

Radiofrequency (RF) is a family of technologies, not one procedure. In some aesthetic devices, energy is delivered through an electrode that remains on the skin surface. The word “radiofrequency” alone does not identify the model, configuration, treatment area or energy-delivery method; those details need confirmation for the actual proposal.

02 · RF microneedling uses needle electrodes

RF microneedles add a penetrating step

With RF microneedling, needle-shaped electrodes penetrate the skin and deliver energy at a planned depth. This combines mechanical puncture with RF and should not be treated as synonymous with a non-invasive surface-electrode application. Nor should readers assume that the radiofrequency service listed at this clinic includes RF microneedling.

03 · A US regulatory warning does not measure every device’s risk

Risks depend on the device and how it is used

In 2025, the US FDA communicated reports of serious complications associated with certain uses of RF microneedling devices, including burns, scarring, fat loss and nerve injury. The communication said its evaluation was ongoing; it does not provide a universal rate or show that every device, use or procedure has the same risk. The FDA regulates products in the US, so its communication does not determine Brazilian authorization or this clinic’s protocol.

04 · Ask what will be used and why

Confirm the actual procedure before comparing

Before deciding, ask whether the technique punctures the skin, which device and applicator will be used, what goal is proposed, which risks and recovery apply, and who is qualified to perform it. See the radiofrequency and microneedling pages; they describe different services and do not confirm RF microneedling.
