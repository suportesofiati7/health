Laser and IPL: what should I ask about eye protection?

Laser and intense pulsed light do not use the same light spectrum. Learn why eye protection must match the device and what to ask before a session.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational guide; local instructions apply

Appearance depends on the treatment mode and plan

CO₂ laser can be delivered in different modes and intensities. Redness, swelling, sensitivity, peeling or drainage may occur during some recoveries; by themselves, they cannot establish either a complication or that healing is proceeding as expected.

Before treatment, request written guidance for the exact device, area and mode planned. Agree who will follow healing and how to get in touch if something concerns you.

Notice changes; do not diagnose yourself online

Contact the treating team if pain is increasing or returns after improving, redness or swelling worsens, fever develops, bleeding persists, concerning drainage or blisters appear, or an area seems not to be healing as explained.

These signs do not identify a specific cause. Post-laser appearance can overlap with infection, irritation or other responses; a professional assessment is needed to interpret the situation and advise you.

When to seek care without waiting for an online reply

Seek urgent medical care for vision changes, eye pain or another significant change involving the eyes, severe general symptoms or rapid worsening. If you are unsure how urgent it is, contact a health service. Do not wait for the next session or rely on a photograph to decide.

The treating practitioner’s aftercare instructions and a health assessment take priority. Do not apply antibiotics, corticosteroids, anesthetics or home remedies without advice; some products may irritate sensitized skin or make assessment harder.

Agree on contact and follow-up in advance

Ask which number or channel to use, when the team responds, when follow-up will happen and what to do outside business hours. Note the treatment date and mode provided, when a change began, and whether it is improving or worsening.

Exact aftercare depends on the treatment performed. The service page does not publish an individual recovery protocol, so the team should provide specific instructions and a contact plan before the session.
