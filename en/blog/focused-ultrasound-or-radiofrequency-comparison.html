Ultraformer MPT or radiofrequency: what to compare

Focused ultrasound and radiofrequency use different technologies. Learn what to compare and why a device name cannot predict an outcome.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Focused ultrasound and radiofrequency are not synonyms

Different modalities deliver energy in different ways

Focused ultrasound concentrates acoustic energy in planned areas; radiofrequency uses electromagnetic energy to heat tissue. Each category includes different devices, applicators and modes. This general distinction helps explain why “ultrasound” and “radiofrequency” are not names for the same procedure, but it does not explain on its own how a specific device will be used.

02 · Comparative studies still have limits

Evidence does not identify one universal winner

A single-centre prospective study compared radiofrequency and focused ultrasound in 36 people, without randomisation and with short follow-up. It contributes to the discussion, but its sample and design cannot establish a universally better technology or predict results for another device, protocol or person. Reviews of both modalities also combine studies with different devices, indications and evaluation methods.

03 · Model, applicator and plan also matter

A device name does not reveal every detail

The name Ultraformer MPT or “radiofrequency” does not specify the model, applicator, settings, area, operator or indication available at a clinic. The Ultraformer and radiofrequency service pages describe the listed services, but an individual comparison must consider the actual proposal, history and alternatives. Do not infer that products are equivalent or promise a lifting effect.

04 · Compare the proposal, not just the technology

Questions for a clear comparison

Ask which concern is being addressed; which modality, model and applicator will be used; what evidence supports that indication; which limits and risks should be explained; what alternatives exist; and how the result will be reviewed. Read the service pages for Ultraformer MPT and radiofrequency. General studies do not replace confirmation of the applicable protocol and authorization.
