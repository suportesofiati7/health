Scalp mesotherapy and MMP: how to clarify the differences

Scalp mesotherapy and MMP are names that do not, by themselves, reveal the technique, substance or goal of a proposal. Learn what to ask before comparing services.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Technique, device and product are different details

The names do not describe the whole procedure

Scalp mesotherapy and microinfusion of medicines into the skin (MMP) are names used for approaches related to delivering substances into skin, but a name alone does not identify a procedure. Route, device, product, area, purpose and manufacturer instructions are separate details. Do not assume the terms are synonyms—or always completely different techniques—without knowing the actual proposal.

02 · Reviews of scalp mesotherapy show varied protocols

Evidence depends on substance, route and indication

A 2023 systematic review of mesotherapy for hair loss included 27 articles with different classes of agents and combinations. The authors noted a lack of standardized regimens and reports of adverse effects, and called for larger controlled studies. A published result for one substance, technique or population therefore does not confirm efficacy for a different formulation or a service whose product is unidentified.

03 · The MMP acronym does not identify one universal formula

What needs confirmation in the local practice

For MMP, the acronym also does not by itself reveal which device or substance a clinic uses, in which area, for what purpose or within which professional scope. Anvisa advises that products intended for injectable aesthetic procedures must not be regularized as cosmetics; the exact product and authorized purpose need to be identified. A general article cannot establish which products are available or a particular practitioner’s authorization or protocol.

04 · Compare the actual proposal, not just its name

Questions for a responsible comparison

Ask which technique will be used, the full product name and route, which goal is being considered, what evidence applies to that combination, and which risks and care steps should be discussed. If hair loss has not been investigated, ask whether medical assessment is also appropriate. Compare the scalp mesotherapy and MMP pages; also read why ingredients change the evidence and questions before considering MMP on the scalp.
