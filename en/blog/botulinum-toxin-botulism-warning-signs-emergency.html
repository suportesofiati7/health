Botulinum toxin: warning signs that need immediate medical care

Brazil’s health authority warns that serious symptoms may appear hours or weeks after an injection. Learn which signs need immediate medical care and what information to bring.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · The alert is rare but potentially serious

A rare complication deserves prompt recognition

Brazil’s health authority, Anvisa, says that in rare cases botulinum toxin can affect areas distant from the injection site and cause serious symptoms consistent with botulism. Symptoms may begin hours to weeks after an injection. This page is intended to help identify warning signs; it cannot diagnose a reaction or replace medical care.

02 · Watch for vision, eyelid, speech, swallowing and breathing symptoms

Which symptoms Brazil’s health authority highlights

Anvisa highlights blurred vision, drooping eyelids, slurred speech and difficulty swallowing or breathing among early symptoms. Severe cases can involve generalised muscle paralysis. This list cannot tell someone the cause of symptoms at home, and the absence of one particular sign should not be used to rule out an emergency.

03 · Seek emergency care immediately if symptoms occur

What to do without waiting for an online reply

If you or someone who received botulinum toxin develops difficulty breathing, swallowing or speaking, a change in vision or a drooping eyelid, seek medical care immediately or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a message reply, a scheduled appointment or symptoms to progress. Emergency clinicians should assess the symptoms and decide what care is needed.

04 · Share the product, date and injection details

Information to bring to medical care

If you can do so without delaying care, share when the injection occurred, the medicine name and brand, lot and expiry date, treated area and who performed it; bring the packaging or treatment record if available. After medical care, suspected adverse events can be reported to Anvisa through VigiMed. For the listed services, see the pages about upper-face botulinum toxin and platysma and lower-face toxin. If warning signs occur, urgent medical care comes first.

05 · How a consultation clarifies the next step

How a consultation clarifies the next step

In Londrina, a consultation can help determine whether the concern calls for aesthetic care, preparation, monitoring, time for observation or a different referral. A responsible recommendation may be to treat, adjust or simply wait.
