Ultraformer MPT or facelift surgery: why they are not equivalent

Microfocused ultrasound and facelift surgery work in different ways. Learn the limits of comparing them and which questions can align your goal with the option being assessed.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Energy treatment does not reposition tissue like surgery

The interventions have different goals and mechanisms

The Ultraformer MPT service is described as a microfocused-ultrasound technology; a facelift is surgery. These are different interventions. Surgery can reposition deeper tissues and remove excess skin, whereas a non-surgical energy procedure should not be described as equivalent or as reproducing the same type of change. The choice depends on the goal and an in-person assessment, not just a photograph or a technology name.

02 · A review of another system does not prove Ultraformer outcomes

Ultrasound research is not equivalent to surgery

A systematic review and meta-analysis of microfocused ultrasound with visualization (MFU-V) included 42 studies and found global aesthetic improvement in some of the people assessed, with meaningful heterogeneity between studies. MFU-V is a specific platform studied in that literature; this does not automatically validate Ultraformer MPT, compare the technology with facelift surgery or predict an individual result. The review also notes variation in measurements, studies and follow-up.

03 · Excess skin and laxity may call for different discussions

Your individual goal changes the discussion

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons describes a facelift as surgery that can address facial and neck aging concerns, including laxity and excess skin. This information distinguishes intervention categories; it does not determine whether anyone needs surgery. If the main concern involves excess skin, a substantial contour change, previous surgery or an expectation of tissue repositioning, an assessment with a qualified plastic surgeon can clarify surgical options. An aesthetic practitioner can explain the listed service and its limits but does not replace a surgical assessment.

04 · Compare options with qualified professionals

Questions to align expectations and options

At a consultation, explain the change you hope to see, the areas that concern you and treatments you have had. Ask what realistic result Ultraformer MPT may aim for in your case, what its limits are, which evidence applies to the model and applicator being used, which alternatives exist and when a surgical opinion may be more appropriate. Also read about the Ultraformer MPT service in Londrina and its comparison with radiofrequency. A webpage cannot determine suitability or replace a consultation.
