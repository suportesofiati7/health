JOURNAL / Laser and technology

IPL and laser: differences, goals and assessment

IPL and laser are different technologies. Learn what changes and which questions help guide an individual assessment.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · One platform can contain different technologies

A product-family name does not identify the resource used

A laser and light platform may accept handpieces that produce different types of energy and have different intended uses. For example, manufacturer Alma describes Harmony XL PRO as modular, with technologies housed in designated applicators. That describes the manufacturer’s models; it does not confirm the device, modules or configuration at this clinic.

02 · IPL, laser and handpiece are not synonyms

Ask for the exact technology and applicator

“Laser and light” can be a broad description. IPL is intense pulsed light, while a laser emits light with different characteristics; a single platform may also support more than one modality. The goal of a session depends on the device and applicator actually selected. A general IPL-versus-laser explainer can clarify the modalities, but cannot identify which one is planned for an individual.

03 · Documentation and regulatory status are specific too

Confirm the model, manufacturer and regulatory status

Anvisa directs readers to its official health-product search to check the status of medical devices. A manufacturer’s listing for a platform does not confirm that the local unit is the same model, includes all advertised handpieces, or has an authorized intended use for its configuration. The clinic should confirm those details before describing specific indications.

04 · Questions for an informed assessment

What to confirm before comparing options

  • What are the exact manufacturer and model of the available device?
  • Which handpiece or modality is proposed for the concern discussed?
  • What is the stated goal, and what does the model documentation say about that use?
  • How do skin, pigmentation history, tanning and sensitivity inform assessment?
  • Where can I check the applicable Anvisa registration or notification?

The platform name does not establish suitability, results or individual safety. Read about the Harmony laser and light platform and the difference between IPL and laser; the local configuration still requires professional confirmation. 05 · Sources and limits

Sources for identifying the platform

The manufacturer describes options in the Harmony XL PRO line; this does not confirm the clinic’s model or handpieces. Anvisa explains how to search health products in Brazil. Neither source establishes the local protocol or individual suitability.
