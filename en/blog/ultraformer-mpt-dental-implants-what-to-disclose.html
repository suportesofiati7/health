Ultraformer MPT and dental implants: what should you disclose?

A dental implant does not by itself answer whether a particular area can be treated. The decision depends on the exact device, its instructions, and whether the implant is in or near the intended treatment area.

Disclose the implant and where it is

Before an Ultraformer MPT assessment, tell the practitioner about dental implants, recent dental work, and any other implanted material. Share the location and, if known, the material and date of placement. This is information for device-specific screening—not a diagnosis or an automatic yes-or-no rule.

The treatment area matters. A dental implant in the jaw is different from an implant elsewhere in the body, and “metal implant” warnings must be interpreted according to the exact device instructions and the planned treatment area.

Why device-specific instructions matter

Focused-ultrasound devices concentrate energy at a selected focal region. The US FDA guidance for aesthetic focused-ultrasound systems recommends that device labeling address avoiding use over metallic implants in the treatment area. This is general US device guidance; it is not a Brazilian rule, and it does not substitute for the instructions for the exact Ultraformer MPT unit and handpiece.

CLASSYS describes Ultraformer MPT as a focused-ultrasound system with different cartridges and modes. That product overview does not answer whether a particular dental implant and treatment plan are compatible. The clinic’s exact model, handpiece, current manual, and planned area need practitioner confirmation.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Which area and depth are being considered, and how close is that area to the implant?
  • What does the current manufacturer instruction for this exact device say about dental or metallic implants?
  • Does the practitioner need information from my dentist or implant record before deciding?
  • If the proposed area overlaps a device warning, what alternatives or decision to defer are appropriate?

Do not infer that a dental implant is harmless or a contraindication based on a different ultrasound device, a generic online list, or this page alone.

Sources and limits

  • US FDA guidance for aesthetic focused-ultrasound systems; general device guidance, not Brazilian regulation.
  • CLASSYS Ultraformer MPT product overview; describes the platform and cartridge options but does not determine a patient’s eligibility.

The exact clinic device, current instructions, treatment area, and local protocol require practitioner confirmation.
