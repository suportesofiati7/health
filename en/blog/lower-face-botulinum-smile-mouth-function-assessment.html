Lower-face botulinum toxin: what should you share about your smile and mouth function?

In the lower face, nearby muscles contribute to expression and functions such as smiling, speaking and keeping the lips closed. Describing how your mouth moves at rest and during expression can make a discussion about toxin more complete.

  1. Pre-existing asymmetry
  2. Changes in speech or lip movement

Smile and contour are different questions

A concern about the neck or jawline contour may involve muscle movement, skin, volume and other factors. At the same time, lower-face muscles contribute to mouth movement. A careful assessment records what a person notices at rest, when smiling, speaking or closing the lips, without assuming one structure explains the appearance.

Share any pre-existing asymmetry, difficulty keeping liquids or saliva in the mouth, changes in articulation, dental treatment or facial surgery, and known neurological conditions. This information does not automatically mean a procedure will or will not be appropriate; it helps the professional understand baseline function.

What research in this area can and cannot answer

A randomized trial published in 2026 followed 80 participants and compared different extents of lower-face botulinum toxin treatment involving the platysma and depressor anguli oris. Researchers assessed tissue displacement, patient-reported experience and tolerability at 15, 30 and 90 days. The most extensive treatment group reported more adverse effects at the 90-day assessment.

The trial evaluates a defined protocol and population. It does not predict results for another product, distribution, indication or person, and it does not establish what this clinic performs. A review of aesthetic complications also highlights that nearby muscles make functional effects, such as smile changes or altered lip competence, relevant. A review does not provide an individual risk rate.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Which movement or feature is being considered, and how was it assessed at rest and during expression?
  • Was any asymmetry, difficulty speaking, chewing or closing the lips present beforehand?
  • Which exact product and indication are proposed? Does the cited evidence match the product, area and goal?
  • Which functional changes may occur, what signs should prompt contact, and how will follow-up work?
  • What alternatives are available, including not proceeding or waiting?

The US BOTOX Cosmetic label includes a specific indication for platysma bands; that does not extend its labeling to other muscles, products or countries. Confirm the current Brazilian label for the proposed product, its indication and the plan with the professional. This page does not teach injection points, doses or technique.

If a change occurs afterward

A new change in smile, speech, chewing or lip closure should be reported to the responsible care team for guidance. Difficulty swallowing, speaking or breathing after botulinum toxin requires immediate medical attention; do not wait for a message reply. Anvisa and Brazil’s Ministry of Health have published alerts about iatrogenic botulism and its signs.

Sources and limits

  • Munoz-Lora et al. (2026): randomized trial of treatment extent with neuromodulator in the lower face.
  • IADVL Academy (2026): narrative review of complications in botulinum toxin and other aesthetic procedures.
  • FDA (2024): US BOTOX Cosmetic label, including the specific indication for platysma bands.
  • Anvisa: alert on botulism risk after botulinum toxin administration.

These studies and labels do not identify the local product, indication or protocol. The professional should confirm current Brazilian information and its relevance to an individual case.
