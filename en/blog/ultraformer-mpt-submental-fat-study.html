Ultraformer MPT for submental fat: what one study evaluated

A study of 30 people evaluated Ultraformer MPT HIFU for under-chin fat over four weeks. Learn what it reported and its limits before applying the evidence to your situation.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Submental fullness can have different components

“Double chin” describes an appearance, not its cause

“Double chin” is a common phrase for the appearance of fullness under the chin. Appearance can involve bone proportions, adipose tissue, skin and other factors; a photograph or procedure name cannot identify which component explains a concern. A study of fat reduction therefore cannot automatically answer questions about every form of submental fullness.

02 · What the 2025 study evaluated

The study used Ultraformer MPT in a specific population

A 2025 study evaluated 30 Asian adults aged 25 to 60 after one HIFU session with Ultraformer MPT and followed participants for four weeks. The authors reported improvement on clinical assessment and satisfaction scales; self-reported satisfaction was 82%. The publication received an editorial correction replacing the image in Figure 3. The data are specific to that group, device, area and follow-up period.

03 · Early findings are not a guarantee or proof of duration

Four weeks and 30 people cannot answer every question

The study was small and short-term, and does not by itself show how results compare with other options or how long they last. An average result in selected participants cannot confirm individual suitability, session count or response for someone with little submental fat, different anatomy or another goal. Authors with an affiliation to the manufacturer also contributed, which is relevant context for interpreting the evidence.

04 · Ask how individual assessment differs from the study

Compare the study design with the actual proposal

At consultation, ask which component of your concern is being assessed, whether the plan aims to reduce volume or achieve another change, what evidence applies to the available device, what uncertainties remain and which alternatives exist. The practitioner needs to confirm the actual model, applicators and protocol; this page does not assume them. Read about Ultraformer MPT and facial volume and area-specific goals.
