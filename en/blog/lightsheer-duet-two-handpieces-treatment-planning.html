LightSheer Duet: what do its two handpieces mean for planning?

The manufacturer describes two LightSheer Duet applicators with different contact areas and comfort features. The platform name alone does not show which configuration is installed or how a session will be planned.

Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational reading

One platform, more than one applicator

In LightSheer Duet product material, Lumenis describes two handpieces: ET, with a smaller contact area and ChillTip contact cooling, and HS, with a larger contact area and vacuum-assist technology. This is the manufacturer’s engineering description; it does not show that one handpiece is always better, more comfortable or more effective for every area or person.

The manufacturer also describes product configurations that can vary. So “Duet” alone does not confirm that both handpieces are available or used at a clinic. The installed configuration, handpiece, applicable Brazilian regulatory identification and instructions for use need local confirmation.

What those differences mean for a consultation

Contact area, comfort mechanism, body area and skin characteristics may be part of a planning discussion. Specifications do not automatically reveal fluence, pulse duration, sequence of pulses or an individual clinical decision. Those parameters depend on professional assessment and the exact device instructions.

Manufacturer marketing can help identify product features, but it does not replace an independent comparative clinical trial or prove that advertised results will occur with every protocol. Do not use catalogue values to select, adjust or operate a laser.

Questions to ask before a session

  • What is the exact model and configuration of the installed device?
  • Which handpiece will be used for this area and why?
  • How do skin tone, hair, tanning and history affect the plan?
  • What risks, alternatives and aftercare apply to your case?
  • How can you check the product’s registration and intended use in Brazil?
