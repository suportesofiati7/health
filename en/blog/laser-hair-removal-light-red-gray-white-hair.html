Laser hair removal for light, red, white or gray hair: what to know

Laser hair reduction relies on hair pigment as a target. Learn what evidence says about light, red, white or gray hair and what needs individual assessment.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Hair color is part of assessment

Pigment is the light target

Laser hair reduction uses light absorbed by pigment in the hair and follicular structures. Hair color, thickness and distribution therefore matter alongside skin, treatment area and device. Appearance can help shape useful questions, but cannot predict an individual result on its own.

02 · Very light hair may respond less

White and gray hair generally provide little target pigment

Dermatology guidance and photoepilation literature describe lower response when hair contains little target pigment. White and gray hairs generally have little or no pigment for laser hair-removal energy to target; very light blonde hair may also be a weak target. Red hair varies and should not be assumed to behave like brown or black hair. This does not justify promising a response or deciding suitability without assessing the treatment area.

03 · The device name does not settle the target question

LightSheer Duet and hair pigment are separate questions

The manufacturer describes LightSheer Duet as an 800 nm diode platform. That identifies a device feature; it does not mean every hair color or thickness is a suitable target. Response depends on the interaction between device, hair, skin and application. General studies and guidance cannot replace the specific device documentation or assessment of the hairs in the area being considered.

04 · What to ask before booking sessions

Discuss the actual area and realistic goal

  • What are the color, thickness and distribution of hair in this area?
  • What can assessment say about target pigment and expected reduction?
  • Which model and handpiece will be used, and what do the applicable instructions say?
  • How do skin color and tanning history affect the safety assessment?
  • What alternatives should be discussed if the hair offers little target pigment?

Ask for an individual explanation before deciding on a package or a number of sessions. Read about LightSheer Duet hair reduction and how skin, hair and device affect assessment.
