Ultraformer MPT and HIFU: what these terms mean

HIFU describes a category of focused ultrasound; MPT identifies a technology in the Ultraformer line. Learn why the terms do not reveal a protocol or prove an outcome.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Technology category and product name

HIFU and MPT answer different questions

Classys describes Ultraformer MPT as a HIFU platform and uses MPT for “Micro-Pulsed Technology.” HIFU stands for high-intensity focused ultrasound; MPT is the name of a specific technology in the Ultraformer line. The terms are not synonyms: one refers to the energy category described by the manufacturer and the other identifies a product feature/name. This terminology alone does not demonstrate effectiveness for an individual indication.

02 · Model, handpiece and intended use still matter

A device name does not describe the full application

Two devices described as HIFU are not automatically equivalent. The name does not tell you which model, handpiece, cartridge, mode, area, intended use, parameters, training or manufacturer instructions will be used. Do not transfer settings, outcomes, risks or claims between devices only because both use the HIFU label. Confirm the device and intended use available at the clinic with the practitioner and applicable documentation.

03 · Studies must match the platform being discussed

Evidence from another platform cannot be transferred

A systematic review of microfocused ultrasound with visualization (MFU-V) examined a different platform. Its findings do not prove Ultraformer MPT performance. A separate study of MPT for the submental area is discussed in the evidence guide about fat under the chin; a specific sample and objective likewise cannot prove outcomes for other areas or people. Evidence must be read alongside the device, indication, study design and limitations.

04 · Straightforward questions for a precise discussion

What to confirm during assessment

During assessment, ask which model and handpiece are proposed, which goal is being considered, which studies match that exact device and indication, what limits and risks have been explained, which alternatives exist and whether the product is regularised for the stated use. Read the Ultraformer MPT service page and use Brazil’s Anvisa health-product lookup. This article clarifies terms; it cannot determine individual suitability, settings or outcome.
