Drooping eyelid after botulinum toxin: how to get assessed

A lower eyelid or a feeling of heaviness after toxin can have different explanations, including a change in the brow or in the eyelid itself. A photo cannot distinguish the cause, and trying to correct it yourself is not safe; report when it began and ask for clinical guidance.

A drooping eyelid and a lowered brow are not the same description

A lower upper eyelid (eyelid ptosis) and a brow that appears lower (brow ptosis) can both feel heavy or change how open the eye looks, but they involve different structures. Other eye or neurologic conditions can also change eyelid position. Assessment considers movement, position, symptom onset, vision and history; a single image cannot establish the cause.

The American Academy of Dermatology describes temporary weakness in a nearby muscle, leaving the brow or eyelid droopy, as a rare adverse effect of botulinum toxin. The US BOTOX Cosmetic label also lists eyelid and brow ptosis in studies of specific indications. This does not establish a rate for a different product, dose, area or local practice.

What if the change appeared after treatment?

Contact the professional who performed the procedure and ask for an assessment. Share when the treatment took place, which areas were treated, when the change began, whether it is progressing and whether there is double vision, blurred vision, pain, pupil change or other symptoms. If you have the product name and label, share them; units and results should not be compared between brands as if they were interchangeable.

Do not massage the area, start new eye drops or medicines without advice, or get an additional injection to try to raise the eyelid. The approach depends on the cause, visual function, product and professional assessment. Duration also varies; do not use the timeline from one study or product to predict your case.

When should you seek urgent care?

Seek urgent medical care for sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, new double vision, unequal pupils, significant weakness or rapid worsening. Brazil’s Anvisa also advises immediate care for blurred vision, drooping eyelids accompanied by slurred speech, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or generalized weakness after botulinum toxin. Do not wait for a cosmetic follow-up to assess symptoms that may be an emergency.

An isolated drooping eyelid does not confirm systemic spread of toxin, but it should not automatically be dismissed as a simple cosmetic reaction either. A health professional needs to assess the situation in context.

Useful information to bring

  • Treatment date, indication and area; brand and batch if available.
  • Before-and-after photos taken in similar positions and lighting, if available without delaying assessment.
  • Whether the change seems to involve the brow, eyelid margin or both—without trying to diagnose it.
  • Any change in vision, pain, pupil, speech, swallowing, breathing or strength.
  • Eye conditions, surgeries, medicines and previous episodes of ptosis.
