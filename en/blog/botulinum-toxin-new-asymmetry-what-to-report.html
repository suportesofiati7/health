After botulinum toxin: what should you report about a new asymmetry?

A change noticed after botulinum toxin needs context, not self-correction. Organize when it began, what changed and which product details to share at follow-up.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Describe the change, not its cause

Explain what you noticed without trying to diagnose it

A difference between sides may have been present before treatment or may become more noticeable when facial expression changes. If you notice a new change, record when it appeared, whether it occurs at rest or with movement, whether it is stable or progressing, and which areas seem involved. A photograph may help the discussion if your clinician requests one, but it cannot establish what happened by itself.

02 · Bring treatment details

Product and history help the team advise you

Note the date, reason for treatment, areas treated, medicine brand, lot and expiry date if available, as well as previous injections and effects you noticed. Anvisa says patients should have product information and that injection history should be considered. There is no universal time point for interpreting every asymmetry without knowing the product and individual situation.

03 · Contact the team that treated you

Ask before trying to correct the change

Contact the professional who performed the procedure and describe the change and how it has progressed. Ask which follow-up channel to use, when the team should reassess you, and which signs require faster care. Do not massage the area, try home remedies or book another injection to compensate without an assessment. Product, indication, interval and next steps require professional review.

04 · Recognize urgent symptoms

Some symptoms need immediate medical attention

Anvisa advises seeking emergency care for blurred vision, drooping eyelids, slurred speech, or difficulty swallowing or breathing after botulinum toxin. Do not wait for a message reply or routine visit: go to an emergency department and say you received a toxin injection; bring medicine details if available. For related warning signs, see the urgent-care guide.

05 · References and limits

Sources consulted

Anvisa’s alert applies to botulinum-toxin medicines as a class; the label, use and advice depend on the specific product. This article does not diagnose a complication or replace contact with the treating team or emergency care.
