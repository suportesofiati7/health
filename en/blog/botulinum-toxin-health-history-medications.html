Before botulinum toxin: what health and medication details should you share?

An informed conversation starts with your full history. Health conditions, allergies, medicines and recent changes help the practitioner consult the label for the proposed product and decide what needs clarification before you make a decision.

Your history supports assessment; it does not decide the answer by itself

Share relevant diagnoses, muscle weakness or neurological conditions, swallowing or breathing difficulties, allergies and reactions to prior procedures. Mention pregnancy, breastfeeding or the possibility of pregnancy so the practitioner can check the guidance that applies to the medicine being considered. A webpage cannot determine eligibility: that depends on assessment and the current label for the specific product.

Bring a complete list of medicines and products

Write down prescription and over-the-counter medicines, supplements, recent treatments and dose changes. Some botulinum toxin product labels describe interactions or caution with medicines that affect neuromuscular transmission and muscle relaxants. This does not mean every combination is prohibited; the practitioner needs to review the medicine, label and your clinical context.

Do not stop, delay or change a medicine to prepare for a consultation or procedure without speaking with the prescriber and responsible practitioner.

The exact product changes what must be checked

Botulinum toxin products have their own labels and indications. A 2024 Brazilian safety letter about one specific product describes that product’s conditions, warnings and interactions; it should not be turned into a universal checklist for every brand. Anvisa reinforces the use of registered medicines, following the product label and informing patients about brand, lot and expiry date.

You can ask which medicine is proposed, where to find its approved label and which parts of your history need discussion. If you do not know a medicine’s name, bring the packaging or a reliable list rather than guessing.

Useful information to bring to the discussion

  • Your current medicine, supplement and known-allergy list.
  • Relevant neurological, muscular, breathing or swallowing diagnoses, if any.
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding or the possibility of pregnancy, for review against the product label.
  • Recent procedures, prior reactions and any botulinum toxin injections you have received.
  • Questions about the proposed medicine, indication, risks, alternatives and follow-up.

You do not need to post private health details on a public webpage; share them privately during the consultation.

When the conversation needs more time

If a health or medication detail is unclear, ask for it to be reviewed before deciding. Delaying a decision while a question is clarified is something to discuss, not a rule this page can impose. New or urgent symptoms should be assessed by a health service rather than waiting for an aesthetic appointment.

Official sources and limits

  • Anvisa: botulinum toxin warning, product labels and patient information
  • Anvisa: health-professional letter about one specific product (Botulift®, 2024)
  • Anvisa: approved label search for the product cited in the letter

The letter concerns one product and does not establish rules for all toxin products. The clinic does not publicly identify the medicine intended for use; the practitioner should confirm the applicable label and configuration before discussing specific recommendations.
