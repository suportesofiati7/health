Laser hair removal: how skin, hair and device affect assessment

Hair colour and thickness, skin tone, treatment area and laser type affect response. Learn what to discuss before deciding.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Hair colour, thickness and growth cycle

The target and hair characteristics

Laser hair removal depends on how light interacts with pigmented structures in hair. Hair colour and thickness, body area and growth phase affect the response. Since hairs are not all in the same phase at the same time, sessions may be spaced over time; an exact number should not be promised before assessing the person and reviewing their response.

02 · Skin tone and technology must be considered together

Skin and device also shape assessment

Skin tone matters because energy also interacts with skin pigment. Appearance or a simple chart cannot determine on its own whether someone can have treatment. The AAD notes that different skin tones may be considered with appropriate care, and that hair colour and thickness, treatment area and laser type affect results. Operator experience and the actual device also matter.

03 · Individual responses vary

Variation does not mean guesswork

“Laser works for everyone” and “it cannot be used on darker skin” are both overgeneralizations. Technology and care have advanced, but unsuitable laser treatment can cause burns, pigment changes or scars. An individual assessment should consider recent tanning, a history of pigmentation or scarring, medicines, sensitivity and goals. Share this information; do not change medicines on your own.

04 · Questions to bring to a consultation

Questions for an informed decision

Ask which device and laser type will be used, how the team assesses your skin tone and hair, what outcomes are reasonable for you, how many sessions might be considered before reassessment, which risks should be discussed, and what preparation is recommended. The laser hair removal preparation guide covers the pre-treatment conversation; the LightSheer Duet service page describes the clinic’s offered option.
