CO₂ laser and cold-sore history: what should you share?

A history of cold sores is worth sharing before a conversation about facial laser resurfacing; medication decisions require the treating practitioner.

By Franciele Sofiati · CRBM 6277Educational clinical reading

01 · Share your history clearly

Cold sores and previous outbreaks are relevant details

Mention whether you have had cold sores, when the most recent episode occurred, whether outbreaks recur and whether a procedure was followed by lesions. Also disclose an active lesion, recent symptoms, and health conditions or treatments that may affect your health. You do not need to know the virus type to describe what happened.

02 · Why ask before resurfacing

Literature describes herpes reactivation after ablative laser procedures

An expert consensus on fractional ablative CO₂ and a guideline on cosmetic interventions discuss assessment and herpes simplex prevention around resurfacing procedures. These sources do not identify the equipment or configuration at this clinic and cannot define an individual regimen through a webpage. The practitioner should decide based on the specific procedure, history and applicable instructions.

03 · Do not self-medicate or omit an active lesion

Preventive decisions require professional assessment

Do not start, stop or share antiviral medicine on your own. If an active lesion, tingling or recent symptoms occur near the planned date, tell the care team before the procedure and ask for individual guidance. Published recommendations may vary with treatment extent, ablative modality and protocol; they do not authorize transferring a dose, duration or prescription.

04 · Questions to bring to consultation

Confirm what applies to the proposed procedure

  • Does my cold-sore history or a previous procedure-related outbreak change assessment?
  • What should I do if a lesion appears near the planned date?
  • Which CO₂ modality, area and plan are being considered?
  • Is individual prevention indicated, and who should prescribe it?
  • Which contact route should I use if symptoms appear during recovery?
